# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where the claims can be attacked without
attacking the people who make them. Participants are expected to be
rigorous, honest, and civil.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, methods, and evidence — not persons.
- State uncertainty and correct your own errors.
- Report negative results and failed replications.
- Respect confidentiality and consent in human-subjects work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Fabricating or selectively reporting data.
- Presenting a result as confirmatory when it was exploratory.
- Publishing personal data without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who
violate these rules. Report concerns to the repository maintainer.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
